inspection instant it was the only socket holder and there were no established
client connections. The Infinigen orchester confirmed it owns this server; it

`ThreadingHTTPServer`; `DatasetCatalog` caches scans for five seconds. This
indicates the earlier five-second timeout was a cold scan of the `/bean`/`out`
catalog roots (NFS-sensitive), not a hung server. A restart is not warranted
for this observation.
